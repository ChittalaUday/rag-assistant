# Local Niruthi Company Knowledge Assistant
## Intent
Answer company questions from uploaded documents with citations on the left and a document-derived relationship map on the right. Queries focus/highlight supported connections. Clicking an edge opens its exact supporting passage. Run on the user's M5 Pro (24 GB) with Ollama, entirely local application data and inference. Development uses authenticated Claude and Codex tooling with explicitly shared tasks and disjoint file ownership. The active starter corpus is a dated public-source snapshot of Niruthi Climate and Ecosystems; it is not internal company policy. User uploads can extend the local workspace.
## Architecture
One monorepo: Python/FastAPI backend, React/Vite/TypeScript frontend. Parse text/Markdown natively and PDF/DOCX via Docling. SHA-256 hashes deduplicate and detect replacements. SQLite owns documents, chunks, embeddings, validated entity relations and conversation history. Background worker serializes extraction to bound model load. Ollama qwen3.5:9b handles structured extraction and answers; all-minilm:l6-v2 embeds short overlapping chunks. The engine evaluation selected application-owned SQLite retrieval and a validated evidence graph. LightRAG remains evaluation-only because its default generated relationship descriptions do not meet the exact-quote evidence contract. Keep the historical fictional probe and its raw measurements explicitly separate from active Niruthi quality evaluation.
## Evidence and retrieval
Nodes and edge labels derive from open model extraction, not predefined words. Require each extracted relation's quote to occur verbatim in its source chunk, include both explicit endpoint names, and contain the short source-derived relational label; reject invalid relationships and surface warnings. Conservative textual validation may omit implicit/pronominal relationships and does not establish complete semantic correctness. Merge normalized entity names conservatively; deletion removes only that document's evidence and orphaned edges; replacement publishes chunks and source metadata atomically, retaining active evidence when processing fails. Combine cosine embedding retrieval with text overlap and graph expansion. Persist conversation context to resolve follow-ups without treating prior answers as documentary evidence. Generate only from selected citations; warn on invalid cited IDs and abstain for missing evidence. Highlight only relevant source-backed edges.
## UI
Calm light workspace with narrow navigation, answer panel, and spacious graph panel. Document upload/progress/error/retry/delete and Load Niruthi public documents control with a public-source snapshot disclaimer. Validated answer segments delivered through SSE after generation, persisted history, current-source panel with safe original website links, graph fit/zoom, node search, keyboard-accessible relation list and responsive panel switching. Loading, empty, model-offline and malformed-response states must be explicit. No fabricated graph in empty state.
## Quality and limits
Python 3.12+, Node 22+. Bound uploads (20 MB), background jobs, chunk/model context, and model timeouts. Loopback-only serving by default; unauthenticated local single-user tool, not production deployment. Strict linters/type checks, backend behavior tests, frontend component tests and browser integration checks. Six public source-linked Niruthi fact notes and 30 active expected questions, including cross-document, follow-up, source-awareness and unknown cases. Preserve earlier fictional Northstar fixtures under fixtures/archive/northstar/ for historical reproducibility. Record latency/quality observations and model issues. No silent model downloads or invented benchmark outcomes.

## Public-source boundaries and verification
Company marketing statements require attribution. Logos do not establish contracts; certification badges do not establish current validity; NASA framework descriptions do not establish endorsement; government programs do not establish a Niruthi contract. Public notes do not determine internal leave policy, employee salaries, current headcount or annual revenue. Missing evidence requires abstention. Source manifest and snapshot date live in fixtures/sources.json and docs/reports/public-sources.md. Loading the snapshot does not fetch or continuously synchronize websites.

Bound prompts conservatively with output reserve, compact role/content history, and whole selected evidence records. Job timeout failures must mark the current document failed and leave the worker available for later jobs. Source/history navigation must ignore stale asynchronous responses and drawer-close/new-chat invalidation. Model evaluation records actual responses and manually reviews claim/source alignment; term/source coverage is only a smoke metric. Browser checks remain permission-dependent and must not bypass a desktop approval denial.
