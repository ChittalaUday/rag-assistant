# Public programs and recognition

Snapshot: 2026-10-05
Source: https://www.globalinclusiveinsuranceforum.org/multimedia/niruthi-how-does-niruthi-uses-technology-improve-livelihoods-smallholder-farmers
Source: https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2085179

The World Bank Group GIIF interview dated 31 August 2021 describes Niruthi as an agritech business supporting crop monitoring and yield forecasting. Interviewees were Cristina Milesi, then described as Director, and Mallikarjun Kukunuri, then described as Chief Executive. GIIF reports a Gold Award in the Data & Analytics category at the 2019 Insuretech Innovation Challenge in Mumbai. It discusses smallholder farmers, financial institutions, insurers and government agencies.

The Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare PIB release dated 17 December 2024 describes YES-TECH, Yield Estimation System Based on Technology, under PMFBY from Kharif 2023. It refers readers to the YES-TECH manual for Technology Implementation Partners. This release does not name Niruthi. Program existence alone does not establish a Niruthi contract or empanelment.
