Company marketing claims are not independently verified by this collection. Logos do not establish client contracts, and displayed certification badges do not establish certification validity. NASA's TOPS description is not a Niruthi endorsement; the reviewed PIB YES-TECH release does not name Niruthi.

Current headcount, annual revenue, employee salaries and leave policy are not established by reviewed public sources. This is a limit of the collection, not a claim that the company lacks that information. Policy publication dates and source retrieval dates have different meanings.
