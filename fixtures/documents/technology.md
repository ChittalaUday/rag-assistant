Niruthi says its satellite and ground-data approach is derived from NASA TOPS, the Terrestrial Observation and Prediction System.

NASA Earthdata describes TOPS as combining ecosystem simulations with satellite measurements and surface weather observations to generate ecosystem nowcasts and forecasts. Its program page lists Ramakrishna Nemani as Principal Investigator at NASA Ames Research Center and places deployment at NASA Earth Exchange. Applications discussed include natural-resource management, public health and disaster management.

The NASA page does not reference Niruthi. It explains a research system, while Niruthi supplies the commercial-technology linkage. The source does not establish NASA endorsement of Niruthi, employment today or a license agreement.
