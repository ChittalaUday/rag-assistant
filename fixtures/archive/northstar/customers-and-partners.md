# Customers and Partners

> Fictional reference document. All customer, partner, and contact names are invented for test fixtures.

Northstar Signal's customer and partner relationships, with named accounts and primary contacts. As of 2026-10-01.

## Named customers

- **Mercator Shipping** — maritime fleet operator, Rotterdam. Approximately 3,200 Beacon units across 180 vessels. Primary CSM and executive sponsor: Aisha Patel. Contract renewed 2026-01-12. Uses Pulse with the OrbitLink satellite comms integration for over-water coverage.
- **Parallax Grid Co.** — regional electric utility, Austin. Approximately 2,100 Beacons on substations and switchgear. Primary CSM: Chen-Wei Lin. Requires 99.95% monthly uptime per contract. The Pulse multi-region project is driven by their RPO requirement.
- **Ironhide Mining** — open-pit mining operator, Perth. Approximately 1,500 Beacons on heavy haul vehicles. Onboarded via Delta-V Integrators in 2024. Primary CSM: Jamal Osei. Runs a non-standard CrowdStrike policy exception on their on-prem bastion, reviewed quarterly by Security.
- **Volans Logistics** — mid-market freight. Approximately 450 Beacons. Primary CSM: Sofia Marín.
- **Bluefin Oil & Gas** — offshore platforms, North Sea. Approximately 900 Beacons. Primary CSM: Jamal Osei. Procurement contact: Nils Haugen.

ARR concentration: Mercator, Parallax, and Ironhide represent approximately 61% of total ARR as of Q3 2026.
